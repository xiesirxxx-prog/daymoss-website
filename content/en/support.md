## Contact

For help, feedback, or privacy questions, email [developer@yunostudio.cc](mailto:developer@yunostudio.cc).

Support is provided by Daymoss’s developer and operator; no fixed reply time is guaranteed.

## Supported devices and availability

Daymoss 1.0 supports iPhone and requires iOS 26.5 or later. The initial release is intended for selected English-speaking markets outside mainland China. A Chinese help page does not indicate mainland China availability. Check your local App Store listing for availability.

Version 1.0 does not require an account or sign-in and has no subscriptions or in-app purchases.

## Where are my journals? Do they sync?

Journal text and re-encoded image copies are stored in the local Application Support directory within Daymoss’s sandbox. Daymoss does not upload them to a developer-operated server or provide its own cloud sync. It contains no analytics, advertising, or third-party crash-reporting SDKs.

Your device’s iCloud or computer backup may include app data depending on your settings. A device backup is not continuous Daymoss sync. Before changing phones or deleting the app, check your backup arrangements. The developer cannot recover journals from a server, and successful backup restoration is not guaranteed.

## Photos and the camera

Use the system photo picker to choose an image. Daymoss receives the images you select through PhotosPicker, rather than unrestricted access to your photo library. If an image is only in iCloud, the system may need internet access to retrieve it. If selection fails, check that the image is available in Photos and try again.

Camera capture is optional. If it is unavailable, check Daymoss’s camera permission in iOS Settings. Denying camera access does not grant the developer access to your content.

Images are re-encoded for use in journals. This does not guarantee that every kind of photo metadata is removed. Original images in Photos are separate from Daymoss’s local copies.

## Motion effects, fonts, and internet access

Daymoss uses CoreMotion device-attitude information for visual effects. It does not upload that information to a developer-operated server. If an effect does not behave as expected, include your device model and iOS version in your report; do not assume a specific permission switch is available on every device.

Apple’s on-demand fonts may require a download. Photo retrieval, backups, opening these help pages, and sending email may also need internet access. Local journal storage does not mean the app and all related system services are entirely offline.

## How do I remove local data?

Daymoss 1.0 cannot delete the last remaining journal inside the app. To remove the app’s local sandbox data, open iOS Settings, go to General, then iPhone Storage, select Daymoss, and choose **Delete App**. System labels may vary by language or iOS version.

Do this only after considering whether you need to preserve your journals. **Offload App** keeps documents and data. Removing an icon from the Home Screen alone does not delete the app’s stored data.

Deleting Daymoss does not remove original images from Photos, support emails, or copies in historical device backups. Manage those separately. Restoring an older backup may restore journal data. No account deletion is needed because Daymoss has no accounts.

## Report a problem without exposing private content

- Describe what you were trying to do, what happened, and how to reproduce the issue.
- Include the Daymoss version, iPhone model, and iOS version if available.
- Attach a screenshot only if helpful, after hiding private journal text, images, and other personal details.
- Never send passwords, Apple Account credentials, or an entire device backup.

For a crash or missing content, first reopen the app and record what you see. Do not delete and reinstall the app as an initial troubleshooting step: deleting it removes local data. Email support before taking destructive steps if you need to preserve content. The developer may ask for additional relevant details but cannot guarantee recovery.

Support email and voluntarily supplied details are handled as explained in the [Privacy Policy](/daymoss/privacy). You can use the same email address for access, correction, or deletion requests concerning information held through support.
