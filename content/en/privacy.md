## Scope

This policy explains how Daymoss 1.0, this website, and related support services handle your information. In this policy, “we” and “the developer” refer to Daymoss’s developer and operator.

The initial release is intended for selected English-speaking markets outside mainland China. A Chinese translation is provided for convenience and does not indicate availability in mainland China.

## Your journals stay in local app storage

Daymoss stores journal text and re-encoded copies of images in its Application Support directory within the app’s local sandbox. It uses this content to save and display your journals. Daymoss does not upload this content to a developer-operated server, and the developer cannot remotely access your local journals through the app.

Daymoss 1.0 has no account or sign-in, no subscriptions or in-app purchases, and no app-provided cloud sync. It contains no analytics, advertising, or third-party crash-reporting SDKs. It does not send journal content to advertising or analytics services.

## Photos, camera, and motion

- Photos: Daymoss uses Apple’s PhotosPicker to receive images you choose through the system picker. Choosing an image gives the app that selected content; it does not give the app unrestricted access to your photo library. Apple’s photo services may need a network connection to retrieve a selected item that is stored in iCloud.
- Camera: taking a new photo is optional and uses the camera with your permission. You can decline camera access or change it in iOS Settings. Declining camera access prevents camera capture, rather than requiring you to send journal content to the developer.
- Image processing: imported images are re-encoded for local use. Re-encoding is not a guarantee that every type of photo metadata is removed. Original images in Photos and any separate copies are managed independently of Daymoss.
- Motion: Daymoss uses CoreMotion device-attitude information for visual effects. This processing is for the on-device presentation of the app, not advertising or behavioral analytics, and this information is not uploaded to a developer-operated server. This policy does not promise a separate permission switch. Available system controls depend on the device and iOS version.

## Apple services and network access

Local storage does not mean every related operation is offline. Apple’s on-demand system fonts may be downloaded when needed. Photo retrieval, device backup, opening web pages, and sending support email may also use a network connection. These system and external services are separate from app-provided cloud sync.

Depending on your settings, iCloud Backup or computer backups may include Daymoss’s local data. Apple’s services and any system diagnostic sharing are governed by Apple’s terms and your system settings. The absence of a third-party crash SDK does not mean iOS never creates diagnostic reports. See [Apple’s Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## If you contact support

If you email [developer@yunostudio.cc](mailto:developer@yunostudio.cc), the developer receives your email address, message, and any attachments or troubleshooting details you choose to send. This is separate from automatic collection by the app. Do not send private journal pages or other sensitive information unless necessary; redact screenshots first.

Sending a support email is voluntary, and not sending one does not affect local journaling. The app does not automatically collect your device model, operating system version, logs, or other diagnostic data. Those details are received only if you choose to include them. Do not send passwords, Apple Account credentials, or a complete device backup.

The developer uses this information to answer your request, investigate reported problems, and handle privacy inquiries. Support email is processed through Alibaba Mail (阿里邮箱) for those replies and for troubleshooting. Alibaba Mail delivers and stores that correspondence. Providing this email service does not give Alibaba Mail access to the other journals stored in the app. Support information is not used for advertising or sold. It may be disclosed where legally required or as necessary to handle a legal claim, subject to applicable law.

Correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably needed to handle the request, related follow-up, and any applicable legal obligations. Retention depends on the nature of the request and those obligations. You can ask for deletion using the same email address; any necessary retention or provider-backup limitations will be explained in the response. The developer cannot delete copies in your own mailbox.

## Retention, deletion, and your choices

Local journal data remains on your device until removed through available app controls or by deleting the app, subject to backups and separate copies. Daymoss 1.0 does not allow the last remaining journal to be deleted inside the app.

To remove Daymoss’s local sandbox data, use iOS Settings to **Delete App**. **Offload App** retains documents and data and is not equivalent. Deleting Daymoss does not delete original photos from Photos, support emails, or copies in historical device backups. Restoring a backup may restore app data. Manage backups separately through Apple’s or your computer’s backup settings. The developer has no server copy from which to recover your journals and does not guarantee that a device backup exists, contains all content, or can be restored successfully.

You can decline to select a photo, decline camera permission, and manage applicable permissions in iOS Settings. Revoking a permission does not itself erase previously saved content. There is no Daymoss account to close.

For personal information the developer holds through support, you may request access, correction, or deletion, and exercise any other rights available under the law applicable to you, by email. The developer may request proportionate information to verify a request. This does not give the developer access to or remote control over local journal files. Where applicable, you may also complain to the relevant privacy authority.

## Security and changes

Local data is subject to iOS sandbox protections and your device security. Protect your device and backups; no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. This policy does not promise additional app-level encryption or a separate app lock.

If privacy practices change, this page and the last-updated date above will be updated. If a change does not take effect on the effective date shown above, that different effective date will be stated. Where required, additional notice or consent will be provided before new processing begins. This policy describes version 1.0 and does not promise future account, sync, or subscription features.

## This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not visitors are signed in to GitHub. This website does not add analytics or advertising scripts. These website hosting records are separate from your Daymoss journal content stored on your device.
